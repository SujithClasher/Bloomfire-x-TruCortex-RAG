# TransUnion CIBIL Bureau Overview & CICRA Regulatory Guide

## Executive Overview
**TransUnion CIBIL** (Credit Information Bureau (India) Limited) is India's pioneer credit information company, maintaining credit records for over 1 billion consumer and commercial records. CIBIL operates under the regulatory supervision of the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** and the **Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act, 2005 (CICRA 2005)**.

## Core Functions of TransUnion CIBIL
1. **Aggregating Credit Information**: Member banks, NBFCs, housing finance companies (HFCs), and microfinance institutions are legally mandated to furnish accurate credit data to CIBIL on a monthly or weekly basis.
2. **Generating Credit Information Reports (CIR)**: Compiling standardized consumer and commercial credit profiles reflecting historical borrowing behavior and payment discipline.
3. **Calibrating Bureau Credit Scores (300–900)**: Formulating proprietary statistical scores quantifying credit default risk to assist lending institutions in sound underwriting.
4. **Providing Enterprise Risk Solutions**: Deploying specialized credit products including CreditVision Commercial Rank (CV CMR), Grameen Score, CIBIL Microfinance Score, TruIQ Analytics Studio, and Field Investigation Waiver policies.

## Regulatory Framework & CICRA 2005 Mandates
TransUnion CIBIL operates under strict statutory standards:
- **Section 20 of CICRA 2005 (Accuracy & Confidentiality)**: Credit bureaus and member credit institutions must ensure that credit data is maintained with absolute fidelity and protected against unauthorized disclosure.
- **Section 21 of CICRA 2005 (Customer Dispute Rectification)**: Every individual is legally entitled to dispute inaccuracies in their CIR. Lenders and CIBIL are mandated to complete verification and update erroneous records within **30 calendar days**.
- **Free Full Credit Report (FFCR)**: Pursuant to RBI directives, every Indian citizen is entitled to one free comprehensive credit report and score per calendar year directly from CIBIL's official portal.
- **Strict Prohibition on Unauthorized Access**: Bureau records may only be accessed when an applicant has executed an explicit digital or physical consent mandate for a lawful credit underwriting purpose.
