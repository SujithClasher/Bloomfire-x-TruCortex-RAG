# TransUnion CIBIL Solutions & Featured Products Master Catalog

## Executive Overview
TransUnion CIBIL is India's pioneer and market-leading credit information company, serving over 1 billion individuals and commercial enterprises under the regulatory supervision of the **Reserve Bank of India (RBI)** and the **Credit Information Companies (Regulation) Act (CICRA 2005)**.

This master catalog outlines TransUnion CIBIL's 7 Core Solutions and 15 Featured Products across the **Consumer, Commercial, and Microfinance (MFI)** financial domains.

---

## The 7 Core Solutions

### 1. Analytics Solutions
- **Scope**: Advanced data science and predictive analytics modeling for credit underwriting, fraud detection, and portfolio optimization.
- **Featured Enablers**: **TruIQ Analytics Studio**, custom risk scorecards, Gradient Boosted Decision Trees (LightGBM/XGBoost), WoE logistic regression, and automated model validation (Gini, KS, PSI).

### 2. Collections Management (Debt Management)
- **Scope**: Optimizing recovery rates and reducing collection costs across early-stage (1–30 DPD) and late-stage (60–90+ DPD) accounts.
- **Featured Enablers**: Debt Management Models (DMM), capacity-to-pay modeling, skip tracing, and contact enrichment.

### 3. Credit Information Reports (CIR)
- **Scope**: Multi-format, standardized credit files aggregating past borrowings, active liabilities, and repayment discipline across all member financial institutions.
- **Featured Enablers**: **CIBIL Consumer Report**, **Enhanced Consumer CIR**, **Enhanced CIBIL Commercial Report**, and **CIBIL Microfinance Report**.

### 4. Customer Acquisition
- **Scope**: Identifying, prescreening, and onboarding creditworthy borrowers with minimal acquisition cost.
- **Featured Enablers**: Prescreen scorecards, pre-approved limit sizing, conversion propensity modeling, and soft inquiry campaign verification.

### 5. Financial Inclusion
- **Scope**: Expanding formal credit to unbanked, rural, and semi-urban populations through specialized risk assessment.
- **Featured Enablers**: **Grameen Score** (rural agricultural credit), **CIBIL Microfinance Score** (JLG/SHG lending), and **Commercial NTC Score** (thin-file MSMEs).

### 6. Application Review Solutions
- **Scope**: Front-end origination decisioning to prevent fraud, verify identities, and accelerate straight-through processing.
- **Featured Enablers**: **Field Investigation (FI) Waiver Policy** and **Identity Verification (IDV / IDVision)** with biometric e-KYC validation.

### 7. Portfolio Management
- **Scope**: Continuous post-disbursement monitoring of consumer, commercial, and microfinance loan books.
- **Featured Enablers**: **Portfolio Review Report**, **Microfinance Portfolio Review**, early warning signals (EWS), and proactive limit management (CLI/CLD).
